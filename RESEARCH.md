# Brand.Studio — Competitive research (37 sites)

Research goal: find the most usable, profitable model for **Brand.Studio** and the features, forms, content and design patterns that world-class sites in the branding niche use. Sites were reviewed for: core offer, interactive tools, lead capture, monetisation, content depth and design.

## Winning concept

**Brand.Studio = free, private, browser-based brand-building studio + high-intent guide library + "Get Matched" lead marketplace for branding agencies.**

Why this beats the alternatives:

| Model considered | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Pure blog / inspiration gallery | Crowded (Brand New, BP&O, Identity Designed own it); low commercial intent. |
| AI logo maker SaaS | Capital-intensive, heavy competition, needs payments + accounts. |
| Agency directory only | Needs hundreds of listings on day one; weak without traffic. |
| **Tools + guides + lead marketplace (chosen)** | Tools pull search traffic on high-volume queries (business name generator, colour palette, brand guidelines template, logo cost, trademark search); guides earn AdSense on high-CPC commercial topics (logo design cost, trademark registration, branding agency); the **Get Matched** brief converts the same visitors into agency leads worth far more than ad clicks. Runs free on GitHub Pages. |

Revenue stack (in order of expected value): 1) agency lead fees & partner plans ($149–$499/mo tiers, pay-per-intro); 2) Google AdSense on commercial-intent guides and tool pages; 3) tool / guide / newsletter / contest sponsorships; 4) donations; 5) eventual sale of the site/domain (top-bar CTA).

## Feature checklist derived from research

- Interactive tools: name generator, palette builder + contrast checker, font pairing, wordmark maker (SVG/PNG), slogan generator, archetype quiz, brand audit, brand guide builder (PDF), trademark search launcher, Studio dashboard with kit progress, import/export.
- Lead generation: 5-step Get Matched brief (service → budget → timeline → business → contact), homepage pre-qualifier, audit "expert review" form, archetype report email capture, exit-intent checklist, newsletter, partner applications, hire-talent form, advertising form.
- Monetisation: AdSense (auto ads + reserved slots), partner plans, sponsorships, contest sponsorship, donations, site/domain sale CTA.
- Trust/compliance: privacy (AdSense disclosures), cookies, terms, disclaimer, trademark & copyright disclosure, editorial policy, about, contact, ads.txt, sitemap, robots, structured data.
- Automation: daily GitHub Action refreshes design headlines; daily prompt and palette rotate automatically; contest brief, month and countdown roll over automatically; form auto-responses.
