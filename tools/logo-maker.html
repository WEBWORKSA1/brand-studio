What makes a wordmark work

A wordmark is a logo made primarily of the brand name. It’s the fastest route to recognition for a new brand because every impression teaches people your name — not an abstract symbol they have to learn.

  • Legibility first. Test at 120px wide and at favicon size. If it breaks small, simplify.
  • Spacing is the craft. Tight spacing feels confident and modern; open spacing feels premium and calm. Uppercase usually needs extra tracking.
  • One colour must work. Your logo will be stamped, embroidered and printed in black. Design in one colour, then add colour.
  • Symbols must earn their place. A simple shape can become a favicon, app icon and social avatar. If it adds nothing, leave it out.
