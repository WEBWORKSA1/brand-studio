How the generator builds names

Professional naming teams work from a small set of construction patterns. The generator uses the same ones, so you can see which style suits your brand before you go deeper.

StyleHow it worksBest for
CompoundTwo real words joined (keyword + evocative word).Clear, descriptive brands that still feel ownable.
BlendFront of one word fused with the back of another.Tech and consumer products that need a unique, short name.
Suffix / prefixKeyword plus an ending like -ly, -ify, -ora or a lead-in like Neo- or Get-.Apps and services; easy to understand.
MetaphorA real word that carries the feeling you want (Summit, Lantern, Atlas).Brands that sell an emotion more than a feature.
InventedPronounceable, coined words built from consonant–vowel patterns.Global brands that need trademark strength.
RespelledFamiliar words with an altered spelling.Getting a short .com — but watch the spelling cost.

Filter your shortlist like a pro

  1. The radio test. Say it once on a call. Could the listener spell it?
  2. The crowd test. Search the name with your category. Are competitors close by?
  3. The meaning test. Check it in the languages of your key markets for unfortunate meanings.
  4. The legal test. Run it through the trademark search launcher in the classes you’ll trade in.
  5. The domain test. You don’t need the exact .com, but you need a domain people will type correctly.
