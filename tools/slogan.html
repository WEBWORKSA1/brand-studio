Slogan vs tagline vs positioning

People use these interchangeably, but they do different jobs. Your positioning statement is internal: who you serve, what you do and why you’re different. Your tagline is the public, long-lived line that sits with your logo. A slogan is usually campaign-specific and changes more often.

Five tests for a great tagline

  1. Is it true? Never promise what the product can’t deliver.
  2. Is it short? Under seven words is a good target.
  3. Is it yours? If a competitor could use it unchanged, it’s too generic.
  4. Does it sound like you? Match the voice from your archetype.
  5. Is it clear? Clever is good. Confusing is fatal.
