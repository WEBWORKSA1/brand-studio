Template 01

The 27-point brand launch checklist

Strategy

  1. One-sentence positioning
  2. Top 3 competitors and how you differ
  3. A brand promise you can keep
  4. Primary audience in one paragraph

Name & legal

  1. Passes the phone test
  2. No negative meanings in key languages
  3. Trademark search in your markets
  4. Domain secured
  5. Social handles reserved
  6. Business registration / DBA matches

Visual identity

  1. Logo: full colour, one colour, reversed
  2. Square icon / favicon
  3. SVG + PNG masters stored safely
  4. Five-colour palette with HEX, RGB, CMYK
  5. Text colours pass WCAG AA
  6. Font pair with licences confirmed

Voice

  1. Tagline
  2. Three voice words: “this, not that”
  3. 25 / 50 / 100-word boilerplates
  4. Words you always / never use

Touchpoints

  1. Website hero explains you in 5 seconds
  2. Email signature and templates
  3. Social images and bios aligned
  4. Card, invoice and proposal templates

Governance

  1. One-page brand guide shared
  2. A named brand owner
  3. Quarterly brand review booked

Template 04

Brand creative brief

  1. Background: who we are, what’s changed
  2. Objective: what this project must achieve
  3. Audience: who we must win
  4. Insight: the truth about them we can use
  5. Single-minded message: one thing to remember
  6. Mandatories: must-haves and no-gos
  7. Deliverables & formats
  8. Budget, timeline, decision-maker
  9. How we’ll measure success

Template 05

Company boilerplate

25 words: [Brand] helps [audience] [achieve outcome] with [product/service]. Founded in [year], [Brand] is based in [city].

50 words: add your differentiator, one proof point (customers, awards, results) and your mission.

100 words: add the founding story in one sentence, key products, where you operate and a link.
