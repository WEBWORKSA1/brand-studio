How to use the Studio

A brand is a set of decisions made once and repeated everywhere. The Studio breaks those decisions into eight small, finishable steps so you never stare at a blank page.

  1. Name. Generate options, shortlist five, say each one aloud, and run a trademark and domain check before you get attached.
  2. Palette. Build a five-colour system — primary, secondary, accent, light and dark — and confirm your text pairings pass contrast checks.
  3. Fonts. Choose one headline face and one body face. Two fonts are almost always enough.
  4. Tagline. Pick a promise you can keep. Short beats clever.
  5. Archetype. Take the twelve-question quiz to define personality and voice.
  6. Wordmark. Set your name in your headline font, add a simple symbol if it earns its place, and export SVG and PNG.
  7. Audit. Score your brand across eight pillars to see what to fix first.
  8. Guide. Turn everything into a one-page brand guide and share it with anyone who touches your brand.
Privacy by design: the Studio does not send your kit anywhere. It is stored with your browser’s local storage. Clearing browser data clears the kit, so export a backup when you’re done.

When DIY stops being enough

The Studio gets you to a coherent, defensible starting point. Once you have customers, investors or retail buyers looking closely, a professional identity system — custom lettering, a symbol designed from scratch, packaging, motion and a full guideline — pays for itself. When you’re there, get matched with vetted studios that fit your budget.
