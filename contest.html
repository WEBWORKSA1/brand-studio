How it works

  1. Read the brief shown above. It rotates automatically on the 1st of each month.
  2. Design a wordmark (with or without a symbol) and a five-colour palette. You can use the free wordmark maker and palette builder or any tool you like.
  3. Upload your work to a public link (Behance, Dribbble, Google Drive, Figma) and submit the form.
  4. Voting opens the first week of the following month. Judges’ score counts 70%, community vote 30%.

Judging criteria

  • Fit with the brief (30%)
  • Originality (25%)
  • Craft and legibility (25%)
  • Palette and accessibility (20%)

Rules (short version)

  • Free to enter; one entry per person per month; 18+ or with guardian consent.
  • Work must be 100% original. No AI-generated final artwork, no traced or copied logos, no third-party trademarks.
  • You keep your copyright. You grant Brand.Studio a licence to display your entry with credit.
  • Void where prohibited. Prizes paid by bank transfer or PayPal within 30 days. Full terms in our Terms.
