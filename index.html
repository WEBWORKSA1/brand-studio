FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Brand.Studio really free?

Yes. Every tool and guide is free without an account. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and optional donations.

Do I own what I create?

Yes. Names, palettes, wordmarks and guides you create are yours. Always run a trademark search before using a name or mark commercially — our search launcher makes that quick.

How does Get Matched work?

You send a 60-second brief. We review it and introduce up to three independent studios or freelancers that fit your budget and timeline. Free for you, no obligation to hire.

Are my tool inputs stored?

No. Tools run in your browser. Favourites are saved in your own browser storage. Only forms you choose to submit are sent to us.

I run a studio. Can I get listed?

Yes — apply on the partner page. We accept studios with a real portfolio and verifiable client work.
