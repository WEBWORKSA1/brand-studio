FAQ

Before you send

Is it really free?

Yes. Partner studios pay Brand.Studio for qualified introductions or a listing. You never pay us, and you’re never obliged to hire.

How are partners vetted?

We review portfolios, client references and pricing ranges, and remove partners who don’t respond to introductions or who receive repeated complaints.

Will I be spammed?

No. Your brief goes only to the (up to) three partners we introduce. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

What if none fit?

Reply to the introduction email and we’ll shortlist again, or tell you honestly if your budget and scope don’t match the market.
